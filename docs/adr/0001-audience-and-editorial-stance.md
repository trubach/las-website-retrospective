# ADR 0001: Audience and Editorial Stance

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-13

## Context

The retrospective could be framed anywhere along a spectrum from a purely celebratory visual museum for alumni/general visitors to an internal-only UX audit deck. Editorial voice determines how candidly navigation trade-offs are critiqued and how much historical context accompanies each screenshot.

## Decision

Design and write the site as a **polished, shareable UX and Information Architecture (IA) retrospective tailored for College of LAS web/communications and campus stakeholders**.

- Pair archival visual presentation (chronological homepage showcase across URL epochs) with substantive, evidence-backed IA analysis.
- Explicitly evaluate UX/IA shifts—such as the evolution and decline of audience-segmented navigation (`"For Students"`, `"For Faculty"`), menu breadth vs. depth, and label clarity—using constructive, professional UX terminology suitable for sharing across campus.

## Consequences

- Copy and data models must support analytical commentary per Design Iteration as well as synthesized cross-era UX findings, not merely screenshot captions.
- Visual polish and accessibility must meet institutional presentation standards suitable for University of Illinois stakeholders.
